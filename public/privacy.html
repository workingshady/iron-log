Privacy Policy

Iron Log · last updated 12 November 2026

Short version. Iron Log is a private, invite-only workout tracker for a small group of friends. Your data is used only to run the app for you. It is never sold, never used for ads, and never shared outside the app. Your journal, set notes and pain logs are visible only to you. You decide what your friends can see, and you can delete everything.

1. Who runs Iron Log

Iron Log is a personal, non-commercial project run by its owner for themselves and invited friends at iron-log.workingshady.workers.dev. Contact: message the owner in the app, or use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.

3. Google user data

Iron Log's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only read your Google account id, email and name to sign you in. We do not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data, we do not transfer Google data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models. You can unlink Google at any time in Account & security.

5. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • Data is stored with Cloudflare (Workers, D1 database, Durable Objects), encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Cloudflare.
  • Voice notes are transcribed with Cloudflare Workers AI. The audio isn't kept after transcription unless you send it as a chat voice note.
  • Passcodes are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 plus a per-user salt and a server secret. Sign-in has a growing lockout after failed attempts.
  • Every request is limited to your own data on the server. Friends' data is only returned when your friendship and their privacy settings allow it.

6. How long we keep data

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleted posts, comments and messages are removed from view right away. Backups of the database, kept by the owner to prevent data loss, may hold copies for up to 90 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Settings → Download everything (JSON).
  • Change what friends see: You → ≡ → Privacy. Mark photos private or friends one by one.
  • Delete logs, posts, photos and messages yourself, or ask the owner to delete your whole account.
  • Unlink Google or revoke connected AI assistants in Account & security. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Children

Iron Log is not intended for anyone under 16.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Important changes are announced in the app.
